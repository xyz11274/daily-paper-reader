## 摘要
大语言模型（LLM）正越来越多地被部署为多智能体系统中的自主智能体，但其执行分布式协调协议的能力仍鲜为人知。尽管AgentsNet——一个用于LLM智能体间分布式协调的基准框架——使此类协调成为可能，但赋予完全的推理自主权往往会在复杂领域中导致不一致或性能下降。我们假设，通过源自成熟算法的符号引导来调节智能体自主权，可以改善协调。为研究这一点，我们引入了\emph{符号引导分类法（SGT）}，它刻画了从开放式自然语言推理到完全规定的算法执行的一系列自主权，其中间层级提供部分伪代码引导。我们的结果表明，中间自主权层级始终优于无引导智能体和完全规定性规范。这些发现将自主权调节确定为基于LLM的分布式协调的一个关键设计原则。

## Abstract
Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly deployed as autonomous agents in multi-agent systems, yet their ability to reliably execute distributed coordination protocols remains poorly understood. While AgentsNet, a benchmark framework for distributed coordination among LLM agents, enables such coordination, granting full reasoning autonomy often leads to inconsistent or degraded performance in complex domains.   We hypothesize that coordination can be improved by regulating agent autonomy through symbolic guidance derived from established algorithms. To investigate this, we introduce the \emph{Symbolic Guidance Taxonomy (SGT)}, which characterizes a spectrum of autonomy ranging from open-ended natural language reasoning to fully prescribed algorithmic execution, with intermediate levels providing partial pseudocode guidance. Our results show that intermediate autonomy levels consistently outperform both unguided agents and fully prescriptive specifications. These findings identify autonomy regulation as a key design principle for LLM-based distributed coordination.
